Answered by the fable at my delegation, agreeing with the measured recommendation: `compiler.fail` aborts the body on both paths (Erlang and GC) — nothing after it runs, none of the body's outputs applied, one failure per body, never accumulated. "The body goes on" leaves the Compiler doc; cell `compiler_fail_aborts_body` fixes it. C1 pieces 1–2 synthesise the failure as a throw to `__bp_body_k`'s frame (per 1085), not a recorded flag.
