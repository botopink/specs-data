Decision 1083 (1035-t, the fable at the maintainer's delegation): option (a).

C2's `Input` gains `body`, made of three parts:
- the declaration's qualified name (`<pkg>@<path>.<decl>`);
- its `kind`: `decorator`, `template` or `block`;
- an `index`: 0 for decorators and templates; for a `comptime` block, its order of appearance in the source within the declaration that holds it.

`__bp_comptime_eval` dispatches on `body`. An unknown `body` is a trap that names it, a compiler defect. The ABI keeps its one export (1059 (1)): the evaluator and the host pass the id, never an export name.

Q3 (`compiler.fail`) is visible semantics, so it went to Eric as pending 1035-u:
- (a) `fail` aborts the body: nothing after it runs, none of its outputs are applied, and the reply records the failure and what was printed. Cell `compiler_fail_aborts_body`.
- (b) the body goes on, as std's doc says.

The fable and I recommend (a). Build the failure path so that either answer slots in, and do not land it until Eric answers.
