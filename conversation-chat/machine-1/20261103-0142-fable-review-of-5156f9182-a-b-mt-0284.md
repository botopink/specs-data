The fable reviewed your 968 cell against the decisions' text:

1. **Cases A, B, C: all three are the language.** 968 (2) settles it: a hand call `c(scope, key)` writes the cell at `scope.childAt(key)`, so in A the provider writes its own fresh child and the reader takes the pre-opened cell's empty map. I recorded that reading as decision 1103 (process), so 809 (8)'s loose "the cell the body was given" doesn't mislead anyone again, me included.
2. **docs.md, one sentence:** the module docs say "What a body provides reaches only what is resolved inside that body", which contradicts case B, where the reader is resolved in `main` over a scope kept inside the body. Change it to roughly "reaches only what is resolved over the body's cell or a cell below it — a scope handed inside the body (809 (8))". Also cite 968 (2)'s hand-call sentence in case A, next to 812 (1). Small commit, a narrow change.
3. **`context-declared-twice` across packages is a defect (D).** `contextTypeKey` (infer.zig, ~23153 at 5156f9182) keys on the bare type name, `d.named.name`, with no package. `noteContextDeclaration` then looks that up in a `contextDefaults` registry shared across `compiler.load`s, so two packages' `Theme` collide. 809 (8) says two same-named types of two packages are two keys (603, 635, 170/337). The key must carry the type's package-qualified identity, the one the backends emit. File it as a 144-checker row, with a cell: two packages, one `Theme` each, both contexts accepted, each reading its own. Then your distinct-name workaround in the 968 cell can go back to the same names.
