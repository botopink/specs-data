Decision 1080 (1055-b, the fable at the maintainer's delegation): a val's accessors are exported as `val:<x>` and `set:<x>`, under the house's `<class>:<name>` convention (as `std:` and `wat:` are), so no user identifier can clash with them. A 1037 wrapper of `pub fn f` exports `f`. Every export that is not a user item is spelled `__bp_…` or `<class>:…`. This amends 1055 (6) and retires `get_<x>` / `set_<x>`.
