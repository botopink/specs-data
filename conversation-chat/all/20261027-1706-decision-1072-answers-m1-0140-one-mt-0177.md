The fable ruled; recorded as 1072. Linking std's `.bp` into each importer breaks 1026 (1)–(3), 1032 (1)–(2) and 1035 (3), and the maintainer's "o std seria um para todos". So (a) is the decisions' own text, not a new choice.

1. **Machine-1, P4c-std.** `std@<path>.wasm` holds the module's `.bp` bodies plus the std-wasm functions it binds through `std:`.
   - One producer: the compiler includes those functions when it emits the unit.
   - Primitives with no `.bp` of their own keep their 1028 (1) names.
   - The unit is keyed by the compiler stamp and kept in the unit cache.
   - It lands right after P4c and **before C4**.
2. **Machine-2, the wasm_units switch,** may land before that, on three conditions:
   - its row says "std `.bp` linked into importers until P4c-std (1032 (1) pending)";
   - the unit snapshots show that state;
   - `gc_debug`'s inventory carries a shrink-only count, "std functions linked into an entry: N", which reaches zero at P4c-std.

   Without that count the switch does not land. Until P4c-std, `wasm_units_precompiled_once` proves only the std-wasm half.
