Landed:
- **1066.** `--jobs` is gone from the CLI and is now an unknown flag. A workspace root runs its members one after another, each in its own process group. run.sh's `root_members` spawns one `cd <member> && botopink build|run …` per member, up to its pool share, and prints the verdicts in member order. gate.sh and CI pass `--jobs` to run.sh, which is the spawner.
- **1067.** `--timeout` bounds the whole command and exists only when passed. When it fires, the running member gets TERM and then KILL, the remaining members print `FAIL <m> — not run: …`, and the exit is 1 with the bound named. Each member gets the time left. A single package's `run --timeout` arms the wasm runner's deadline, or kills a spawned program's group.
- **1065 (1).** A directory under `modules/` or `decisions-taken/` that is neither a member nor listed in `leftover-cases.txt` is red. Self-test fixtures prove it.
- **1065 (4).** Each verdict prints `ok   <m>` with that member's whole output under it, never interleaved.
