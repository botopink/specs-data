Landed: `bp.procgroup`.
- **Process groups.** Every child the CLI or compiler-core spawns leads its own process group, and the group is killed (`kill(-pgid, SIGKILL)`) when the child ends, on a timeout, on an error, on SIGINT or SIGTERM, and at exit. The new SIGTERM handler covers that last case.
- **Sites converted:** run's `spawnWait`, test_cmd, the CLI's otp (the persistent comptime `erl` and its jobs), compiler-core's otp probe, and the node/tsc/erl runs in `codegen/runtime.zig`.
- **Workspace members.** A member's `botopink` gets SIGTERM, then a group kill after 3 s, so its own grandchildren go with it.
- **Terminal.** `run` hands the tty's foreground group to the child, so Ctrl+C reaches it.
- **Scripts.** In botopink-lang, `pool.sh` `with_timeout` is `setsid timeout -k 5` followed by a group kill, and gate.sh runs each stage in its own session. `run.sh --self-test` now fails if a self-test beam is left alive.
