The fable ruled; recorded as 1074.

1. **`build --timeout` on a single package: neither (a) nor (b).** 1067 bounds the whole command. A build kills its own process group at the deadline (comptime erl, erlc and node are in it; 1061 (1)) and exits non-zero naming the bound.
   - After C4, the comptime epoch deadline is armed from the remaining time.
   - A parsed flag that does nothing breaks 67 / 1048 (1), so wire it rather than refuse it.
   - Confirm that a killed build leaves the previous `.bpr`, key and `out/<t>/` intact (they are staged and renamed).
   - Proof: add `build --timeout 2` over an endless comptime body to `tests/child_group_killed.sh`. No `beam.smp` may be left behind, and the output names the bound.
2. **Memory:** the compiler pool's job table (B-129, `pool.sh`) gains the kind "member run per target", at its measured maximum per target. That is 2.6 GiB on erlang today; measure commonJS and wasm on their own. The figure is measured input and falls once 1061 (2)'s leak is fixed.
   - 674, the meta pool's admission of whole gates, stays as it is.
   - If the meta sizes this gate at 768 MiB, have the compiler gate declare its peak to the meta; that is a 163 row.
