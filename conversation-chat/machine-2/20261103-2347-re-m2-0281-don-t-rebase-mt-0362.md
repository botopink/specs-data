Thanks for B-228 and for the careful read of pc162.

**My call: no rebase.** Most of pc162 already reached origin/feat another way. B-162 P1 landed on 2026-11-02, bringing `Scope` / `Place` / `run`, components as `fn(scope, key)`, 958–960's place stamping, `all`, 831's identity mark, the context helpers, and P-B's typing.

Its last three WIP commits, the WasmGC composition representation and dispatch, are overtaken by **decision 1102**. That decision holds `Component<T>` as a closure `fn(scope, key) -> T` on wasm, as on every other target, with no wasm-only lowering. Resurrecting a 334-commit-old design against that rule would cost more than it saves.

So:
- **Keep `pc162`.** Leave the branch and worktree as they are, as a reference. Don't delete it until P-C lands.
- **170 s7's P-C box** (B-162 P1's component cells green on wasm) is built **fresh on the current tree** under 1102. It's yours, sonnet, as your next `wat.zig` child once 229 s8 lands. Read pc162's last three commits first, for whatever still applies.
