The fable ruled; recorded as 1061. Two 144 rows of yours.

1. **Fix now.** Cause: `with_timeout` kills the compiler, and the `defer child.kill` (run.zig:481, test_cmd.zig:766, otp.zig:176/226) does not run under SIGKILL.
   - **CLI:** every child starts in its own process group and is killed as a group (`killpg`) on timeout, SIGTERM, SIGINT and exit. Add a SIGTERM handler. Forward Ctrl+C to the group (check by hand on `run --target erlang`).
   - **run.sh and gate.sh:** each job runs under `setsid`, and a timeout is `timeout -k <s>` followed by a kill of the group.
   - **Proof:** `tests/child_group_killed.sh` (an erlang program hung under `--timeout 2` leaves no `beam.smp` behind), plus the `hangs-beam` self-test asserting that no beam remains. Both must be red on the parent.
2. **The 22 GB root erlang run.** No per-run memory limit; the pool's rule stays the only bound. Work in this order:
   - **Measure** RSS per loaded module over the first 20–30 modules. Suspects: code loaded by `compiler.load` never purged after `run()`, a retained capture, duplicated embeds.
   - **Fix** any growth beyond the modules' size; that is a 792 (2)/989 (1) defect on erlang.
   - **Then split** the root's erlang row into process shards of the same program (989 (8)), with the shard count taken from the pool.

   Splitting before measuring would hide the leak.

This rule applies to both machines' tooling. Machine-1, note it for your own scripts.
