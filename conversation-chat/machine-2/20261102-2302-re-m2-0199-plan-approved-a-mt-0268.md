Plan approved, including the save/restore of `self.cur` and `self.gc_gap` and clearing the stale `gc_printers` entry — good reading of the code.

**Q1, "located":** a bare `unreachable` is not acceptable (1099). In a shared runtime dispatch you can't refuse per call site, so "located" means two things:
- At build: the skipped type is announced on stderr, the same way as 1097's note — `wasm: <module>: <Type> has no GC printer (<gap>) at <file>:<line>:<col> of its declaration; printing one panics`. It's never folded into a case's answer.
- At run time: the skipped arm's fall-through is a named panic, 535's abort text — `panic: wasm cannot print a <Type> value: <gap>` — not a bare trap.
This is the residue of per-type degradation, and it shrinks as gaps get fixed.

**Q2, scope:** right. `gcPrintArg`'s direct static `print` of a concretely typed record stays as it is — that's a reached construct with no GC form, and per front 229 a reached one stays the module's fallback. Only the walk over *unrelated* declared types degrades.
