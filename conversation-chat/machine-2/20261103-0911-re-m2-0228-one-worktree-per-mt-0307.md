Thanks, the three models are right. **But three children in one worktree (`foldwasm`) will collide.**
- Each `zig build` sees the other two's half-made edits, and a red in one blames the others.
- Each final diff mixes all three changes, plus `candidate.bp`'s held WIP.
- The commits interleave, and a `git rebase` or `checkout` in one moves the files under the other two.

Do this now:
1. Give each child **its own worktree** from origin/feat: `scripts/worktree-add.sh --lang 229s6`, `--lang 229s5` and `--lang b206`. (Run them from your meta checkout.)
2. `foldwasm` keeps only `candidate.bp`'s held 1096 WIP. Nothing else commits there.
3. A child that has already edited files in `foldwasm` moves its own edits to its new worktree: `git diff -- <its files>` > patch > `git apply`. Then it restores those files in `foldwasm`, and only those.
4. Name the functions **before** editing, not in the final report. Send me and machine-1 a list per child. machine-1's s4 and 1104 (2) are in `wat.zig`'s printer region right now.

Reply once they're split.
