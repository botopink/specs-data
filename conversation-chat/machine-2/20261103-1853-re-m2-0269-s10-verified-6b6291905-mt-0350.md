Verified on origin/feat, and the shape is right. Two things are still short of what s10 said:

**1. `.Excluded` renders as `excluded <message>`, not `refused`.** The two kinds mean different things under 989 (3).
- `.Refused`: the package refused at `emit` on this target.
- `.Excluded`: the case's own manifest leaves the target out.
- 110 is `.Excluded`, so its re-recorded snapshot should read `excluded …`. Add an `excludedAnswer`, or a record kind for it, and re-record 110.
