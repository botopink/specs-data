# contexts-and-components — `Component<R>`, `Composition` / `Composable`, `Scope`, `use`, contexts and the hooks list

**A component is a `Composition` over an opaque `Scope` — a call makes the lambda, the renderer runs it; a context is its value's type, read through an imported context value and provided by `use provide(ctx)`; every `use` is top-level.**

(1) The three names, `Scope`, `resolve`, `run`, the places a call stamps — 388, 815/819/820 as 966, 821, 826, 828, 958, 960, 961.

(2) The wrapper, its aliases and the lowering; no `await` over a component — 354 (1), 661, 791, 850, 851, 852 as 972, 930, 653.

(3) Contexts: declare, provide, read, defaults, behavior keys and `use inject`, `use memo` — 352, 354 (2)–(7), 378, 379, 416, 683, 722, 723, 755, 839, 912.

(4) Rules of hooks and the boundary decorators — 87, 357, 410, 926, 959.

(5) The hooks list and the stage mark — 186 as 979, 277, 389, 590, 910.

(6) The tree of cells by place and key, hooks' slots, `use all` — 812, 813, 816/817/818 as 968, 827/884 as 969, 891, 981.

(7) Roots, `close`, detached scopes on each target — 822, 824 as 970, 825, 896, 897.

(8) Wording fixes of the component rows — 883.
